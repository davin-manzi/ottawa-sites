About us

Auto repair for the Ottawa area since 2023

Country Auto Repair opened in 2023 on Roger Stevens Drive in North Gower, just south of Ottawa, to give drivers honest service at better rates than the large dealerships.

We keep up with modern vehicle technology and still understand older cars, so we can get almost any vehicle, whatever its age or make, safely back on the road. Our technicians get continuous, in-depth training.
