About us

Time-honoured recipes, local ingredients

We bake from scratch using local ingredients and time-honoured recipes. We bake with butter and local eggs, plus maple syrup, apples and Niagara Region fruit.

From Ottawa, our customers have brought our baked goods, especially our cookies, all over the world. Visit the bakery on Gladwin Crescent or find us at the Ottawa Farmers' Market.

Questions

FAQ

How do I order?

Order online and pick up at the bakery. Pickups are available Wednesday to Saturday.

When is the bakery open?

Tuesday to Saturday, 9am to 5pm.

How much are cookies?

Six cookies are $11.75. A gift box of a dozen is $25.50.

Can you print our logo on cookies?

Yes. Custom printed cookies start at 2 dozen for $120, up to 5,000 cookies. Allow at least one week.

Is there parking?

Yes, free visitor parking in the front lot or on the side of Building A.

Where else can I buy your cookies?

At the Ottawa Farmers' Market at Lansdowne, Sundays 9am to 3pm in the Aberdeen Pavilion.
