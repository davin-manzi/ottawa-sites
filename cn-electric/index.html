About us

A Manotick family business since 1961

Chambers and Nesbitt opened C & N Electric in 1961. In 1995 the company was turned over to Ron Sadler, a licensed plumber who started with the company in 1982. Today Charlie Sadler, a master electrician who joined in 1995, is company president.

We celebrated our 50th anniversary in 2011 and have handled every kind of work for residential, commercial and industrial clients across the Ottawa area. Our electricians have serviced industrial plants and stay up to date on the latest safety standards.
