About us

A family business since 1979

Queensway Door Service was established in 1979 as a multi-service window and door company, founded by Raymond Bourgon (1943 to 2019). We are an Ottawa-based, family-owned and operated business.

We sell, install and repair windows and doors in aluminum, steel, vinyl and wood, for homes and commercial buildings across Ottawa. Drop in and our sales team will help with your questions.

Questions

FAQ

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Contact us for a free estimate.

Do you repair windows and doors, or only sell new ones?

Both. We repair windows, patio, storm and garden doors, screens and locks, and replace glass in-house or on-site.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes. We supply and service aluminum and steel entrances, automatic doors, fire-rated doors and security hardware.

Can you find a replacement part for my old window or door?

Often, yes. We carry a wide range of hardware, including hard-to-find pieces.
