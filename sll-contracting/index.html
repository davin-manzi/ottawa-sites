About us

Arnprior's owner-operated roofer

SLL Contracting was established by its owner and operator, Kelly Nych of Arnprior. After early years in the trade in Alberta, Kelly came home to the Ottawa Valley in 2001 to open his own contracting company.

Today SLL installs and repairs every type of roofing system for homes and businesses across Eastern Ontario. Our crews are fully insured, properly trained and equipped with the right safety gear, and no job is too big or too small.
