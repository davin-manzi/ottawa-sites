About us

Ottawa fences since 1987

AL'S Fencing has been a fence contractor people trust in the Ottawa area since 1987. We supply and install fences and gates for homes and pools, from ornamental iron to cedar, PVC and chain link.

We're Ottawa's wholesale Nuvo Iron dealer, with a large inventory of iron fence panels, and AL'S Fencing is part of the Lexon Group family. Our fencing contractors are experienced and fully insured.

FAQ

What areas do you serve?

Ottawa, Kemptville, Stittsville, Manotick, Carp, Kanata, Nepean, Gloucester, Orleans and Cumberland.

What warranties come with your fences?

PVC fences carry a lifetime manufacturer's warranty. Nuvo Iron and chain link fences carry a 10-year manufacturer's warranty.

Do you build pool fences?

Yes, in iron, chain link, wood or PVC. We also offer D&D Technologies top pull gate latches for child safety on new or existing fences.

Do you sell to contractors?

Yes. We're Ottawa's wholesale Nuvo Iron dealer and contractor pricing is available. Order by email, phone or the quote form.

What do you need for an estimate?

Your address, the material and design you have in mind, the rough linear footage, and up to three photos if you have them.
