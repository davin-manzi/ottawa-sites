About us

Your body shop on Gladstone

Aleks Auto Body Works does collision and body repair for all makes of vehicles, from everyday cars and family vans to aluminum-panel Mercedes and a 1951 MG. Our knowledgeable, friendly staff will get your vehicle back on the road looking new.

Our facility is ARI approved and we're an Ontario Ministry of Transportation structural inspection station. We paint with PPG water-based paint, which is better for the environment, and we can source aftermarket and used parts to save you money.

FAQ

Can I get an estimate online?

Yes. Email photos of the damage to aleksautobody@hotmail.com and we'll send you an estimate.

Do you handle insurance claims?

Yes, insurance claims are one of our services. We also do owner-pay repairs.

Can you fix a cracked plastic bumper?

Often, yes. Many plastic bumpers can be repaired instead of replaced.

Do you work on aluminum panels?

Yes. Aluminum is harder to repair than steel, but we've repaired aluminum doors and fenders, like on a 2016 Mercedes C300.

Where are you and is there parking?

480 Gladstone Ave, downtown Ottawa, two blocks west of Bank St. There's ample parking.
