About us

Small engine parts in Merrickville

Lyle's Small Engines carries new and used parts to get your lawn tractor, mower, chainsaw, snowblower or snowmobile working again. We part out all makes of lawn tractors, so there's a good chance we have the motor, transmission, hood, seat or deck you need.

We sell some OEM parts, but with older equipment aftermarket is often the way to go. We also sell used tractors, some as-is and some fixed and ready to mow, and we buy lawn tractors, working or not.
