About us

A family landscaping business, serving Ottawa since 1996

Elmvale Landscaping & Contractors is a family-owned landscaping company serving Ottawa and the surrounding communities since 1996. Landscaping runs in the family, passed down through the generations.

We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques, from a single interlock walkway to a complete backyard with patio, deck, fence, sod and gardens. Every job gets personal service and a final walkthrough with you.

FAQ

How much does landscaping cost?

It depends on the size of the job, the materials and the design. We give free estimates, so call or send a few details for a price on your project.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Consultations and estimates are free for homeowners across Ottawa and the surrounding areas.

Can you do a complete backyard?

Yes. We do full backyard makeovers, including patios, interlocking, decks, fences, sod and gardens.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the project. We'll give you a timeline with your estimate.

What areas do you serve?

All of Ottawa, including Nepean, Kanata, Orléans, Gloucester, Barrhaven and Stittsville.
