About us

A second home for little learners

My Little Preschool opened its doors on July 14, 2008, and has been serving families in Ottawa South ever since. We offer two main programs, for toddlers and preschoolers, built around play and life skills.

Owner and Director Pouneh Shakeri, RECE, graduated from Algonquin College's ECE program in 2001 and has about 20 years of experience. She also works as a Faculty Liaison at Algonquin College.

FAQ

What ages do you care for?

We run two programs: one for toddlers and one for preschoolers.

How do I get a spot?

Waiting lists are kept through the City of Ottawa Centralized Waiting List.

Can I tour the centre?

Yes. Virtual centre tours are offered weekly, by appointment only.

Are you part of the CWELCC program?

Yes. We take part in the CWELCC program, funded by the City of Ottawa and the Ministry of Education.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:30am to 5:30pm.

Where are you?
