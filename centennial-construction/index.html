About us

Building in Rockland since 1967

Gérard Filion founded Centennial Construction in Rockland in 1967, building stables for local farmers. In 1972 he built the Rockland Arena, today the Jean-Marc Lalonde Arena.

Three generations later, the family still runs the company, and customer service is still the top priority. Big job or small, you get personal service from people who know the area.

Questions

FAQ

Do you deliver concrete for small jobs?

Yes. We deliver concrete, cement and mortar to your door, whatever the quantity or use.

Do you deliver on Saturdays?

Concrete delivery on Saturdays is available on request.

What can your concrete be used for?

Foundations, slabs, floors, curbs, in-ground pools, terrace walls and retaining walls.

Can I rent a truck or backhoe?

Yes. We rent tri-axle trucks and backhoes, with skilled operators.

How much gravel do I need?

Multiply length by width in feet and by thickness in inches, divide by 12, then by 27 to get yards. Add one-third for gravel to allow for compaction. Or call us and we'll help.

Where do you work?

Construction in Rockland, Ottawa and Prescott-Russell. Delivery also covers Gatineau and the Outaouais.
