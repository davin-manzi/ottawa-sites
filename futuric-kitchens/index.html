Questions

FAQ

What is cabinet refacing?

We keep your cabinet boxes and layout, replace the doors and drawer fronts, veneer or laminate the visible surfaces, and add new trim and hardware.

How much can refacing save?

Kitchen restyling can save up to 50% of the cost of a full renovation.

Which countertop should I choose?

Laminate is the most affordable with the most colours. Granite resists heat and scratches. Quartz is non-porous and stain resistant, but doesn't handle heat like granite.

Can I see door samples?

Yes. We recommend viewing a sample door in person for exact matching.

How do I clean my new doors?

Use a damp cloth with mild soap. Avoid abrasive cleaners and steel wool, which can void the warranty.
