About us

A four-generation Ottawa roofing family

G. Mongeon Roofing & Contracting has been roofing Ottawa since 1985, with four generations of experience in the trade, and is led today by Danik Mongeon. We repair, restore and maintain flat and shingle roofs on homes, businesses and industrial buildings.

We also install eavestroughs and siding, clear snow and ice from rooftops, and run a quick, effective 24-hour leak service.
