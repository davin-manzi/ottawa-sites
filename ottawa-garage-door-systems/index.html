About us

Locally owned, serving Ottawa for over 30 years

Ottawa Garage Door Systems is a locally owned and operated garage door company. We install new garage doors and automatic openers, and repair and maintain the ones you have.

We work with several manufacturers, so you get a wide range of designs, styles and price points. Our team has over 30 years of management, sales and technical experience, and we stand behind our products.
