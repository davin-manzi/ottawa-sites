About us

Esthetics with experience you can feel

Since 1987, Facial Angle has provided top-quality esthetic services in the same central Ottawa location near Dow's Lake. More than 38 years later, owner Sylvie Lapointe still has the privilege of practising this craft for loyal clients.

Sylvie was president of the Consulting Committee of the Esthetic Program at La Cité Collégiale from 1999 to 2013 and taught at the college in 2016. Every visit gets a customized approach, chosen for your skin.

Questions

FAQ

How do I book?

Book online any time, or call 613-238-2035.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, when we can. If we're not free, we'll happily book you for another time.

Which facial should I book online?

If you're unsure, book the standard facial. Sylvie will choose the best treatment for your skin at your appointment.

How do I prepare for waxing?

Avoid body lotions or oils on the day, and skip retinol products for five days before facial waxing.

What payment do you accept?

Visa, MasterCard, debit, cash and cheques.

I can't make my appointment. What should I do?

Call 613-238-2035 and we'll help you reschedule.
