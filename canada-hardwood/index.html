Questions

FAQ

How much does refinishing cost?

Sanding and refinishing averages $1.50 to $2.00 per sq ft, including 3 coats of finish. A recoat averages $0.65 per sq ft.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Estimates are free and there's no obligation.

Is your work guaranteed?

Yes. Labour comes with a 2-year written guarantee, and flooring carries a 20 to 30 year manufacturer warranty.

Should I choose solid or engineered wood?

Solid wood can be refinished many times and can last over 100 years. Engineered wood has a real wood top layer, and some can be refinished; it can last 20 to 30 years.

Are your workers covered?

Yes. All employees are covered by WSIB and every job site is fully insured.

Do you do stairs and railings?

Yes. We refinish and reface stairs and railings, and install baseboards and quarter round.
