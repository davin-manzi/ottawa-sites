About us

Ottawa's family locksmith since 2001

A Everest Locksmith is a family-owned company that has served Ottawa and the surrounding area since 2001, from our shop at 1581 Bank Street.

We handle residential, commercial and automotive work: lockouts, rekeying, new locks, car keys and safes. Our technicians average over 15 years of experience, and every one is licensed, bonded and insured.
