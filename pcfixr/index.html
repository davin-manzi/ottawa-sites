Questions

FAQ

Which areas do you cover?

Ottawa and Gatineau. Remote support works wherever you are.

How quickly can you get to me?

Our aim is to have someone with you within two hours of your booking.

Are all repairs done at my home?

We do 95% of repairs at your home or business, so you can watch and ask questions. Only in exceptional cases do we take a computer away.

What does it cost?

There's a $40 estimate fee when we arrive, fully refunded if we do the work. On-site work is $75 for the first hour or part and $60 for each additional hour.

'No fix, no fee': where's the catch?

There is no catch. If we can't repair your computer, you won't be charged anything.

Do you fix printers?

We can usually deal with software-related printer issues, but we don't repair printer hardware faults.
