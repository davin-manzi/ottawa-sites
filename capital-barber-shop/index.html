About us

New school feel, old school techniques

Capital Barbershop gives Ottawa a level of barbering above the usual quick cut. Owner Ankur started cutting hair at 14 and studied at Algonquin College.

Step into our vintage-style shop on Bank Street, grab a drink, and sit down with one of our barbers. As Ankur puts it: "We strive to give great haircuts and even better service."

FAQ

How do I book?

Book online any time through our booking page. It takes under a minute.

How much is a haircut?

A haircut is $45, or $60 with a beard trim. Students and seniors pay $40, and kids pay $30.

Where are you located?

590 Bank St, Ottawa, ON K1S 3T3, in the Glebe.

What are your hours?

Tuesday and Wednesday 9am to 6pm, Thursday and Friday 9am to 7pm, Saturday 10am to 6pm, Sunday 10am to 5pm. Closed Mondays.

Do you host wedding parties?

Yes. Our groomsmen package reserves the whole shop for your party, with cuts and beard work for everyone.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, we're a dog-friendly shop.
