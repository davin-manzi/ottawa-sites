About us

Four generations of floor men

Ed's Flooring was founded in 1954 by Eddy Desjardins and has been bringing quality hardwood flooring services to Ottawa ever since, transforming thousands of floors across the city and the surrounding areas.

Alain, a third-generation floor man with over 45 years of experience, has spent his career perfecting the craft. Today Scott, the family's fourth-generation floor man, owns Ed's Flooring. The pride and workmanship passed down from generation to generation continues on every job.

How much dust will there be?

Very little. All our sanding equipment is hooked up to a dustless system with HEPA filters, and we guarantee 95% of the sanding dust is captured.

Yes. We do installation as well as sanding, refinishing, deep cleaning and re-coating.
