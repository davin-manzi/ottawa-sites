About us

Hair as a personal, creative art

Hair by Adamo is a unique, personal and creative expression of the art that is hair. Our focus is keeping your hair healthy while we cut, style and colour it to suit your lifestyle.

We are a Davines Concept Salon in Nepean, with a team of junior stylists, stylists, senior stylists and Director Adamo, so you can choose the stylist and price that fit you.

    FAQ

    How do I book an appointment?

    Call 613-234-6611 or email hairbyadamo@gmail.com with your personal and work numbers, a few preferred dates and times, and the services and stylist you'd like. We reply within 24 to 48 hours.

    How much is a haircut?

    A cut and blow dry is $65 to $140 depending on your stylist's level. Men's cuts are $40 to $65.

    Why do prices differ between stylists?

    Prices follow the stylist's level: junior stylist, stylist, senior stylist or Director. Pick the one that suits you.

    Do you do hair extensions?

    Yes. We offer Great Lengths and Hot Heads extensions by consultation, and extension removal for $100.

    Where are you and when are you open?

    60 Camelot Drive, Nepean. Monday 10am to 3:30pm, Tuesday to Friday 9:30am to 6pm, Saturday 9am to 4pm, Sunday 10am to 3pm.
