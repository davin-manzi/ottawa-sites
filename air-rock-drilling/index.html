Questions

FAQ

How often should I test my well water?

Test for bacteria at least once a year, and after any repairs to the well system.

Should the top of my well be visible?

Yes. A well must always be visible for easy access, with the casing at least 40 cm above ground and a vermin-proof cap.

I have an old well I don't use. What should I do?

Have a licensed well technician abandon it so it can't contaminate other wells. We abandon drilled and dug wells.

Can you check a well before I buy or sell a home?

Yes. We can flow test your well to find the gallons per minute, inspect it and give you a report.

Is there a warranty on pumps and tanks?

Yes. Our pumps and tanks have a 5-year extended warranty plan.

Do you give estimates?

Yes, free estimates. Contact us to schedule an appointment.
