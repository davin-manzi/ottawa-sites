About us

Let us make you beautiful

Mama Cee has been in business since 1999. We're a one-stop shop for beauty in Ottawa: a beauty supply store, a women's salon and a barber shop in one place on St. Laurent Blvd.

We're the largest beauty supply store in Ottawa, with over 100,000 products including wigs, extensions, hair care, skincare and make-up. Not sure what suits you? Our knowledgeable staff will help you out.
