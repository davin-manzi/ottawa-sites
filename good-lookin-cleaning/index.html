Questions

FAQ

How much does carpet cleaning cost?

Our minimum charge is $175 plus tax. Additional bedrooms are $40, staircases $30, and basements or large rooms $40 to $80.

What cleaning method do you use?

Hot water extraction, often called steam cleaning, with a residue-free cleaning solution.

How often should I clean my carpets?

For households with kids or pets, every 4 to 6 months is recommended.

Can you get rid of pet odours?

We offer added deodorizer at $25 per room and pet care special treatments. Ask us about them.

Do you clean businesses?

Yes. We offer daily, weekly, monthly and yearly commercial plans, hard floor care and window cleaning, and we can work after hours or on holidays.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Call 613-836-9847 for a free estimate.
