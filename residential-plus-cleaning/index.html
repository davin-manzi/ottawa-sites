About us

Ottawa's trusted exterior and interior cleaning pros

GnR Residential Plus Cleaning is locally owned and operated in Ottawa. Since 2023 we've delivered full-solution cleaning, clear communication and fair pricing across Ottawa and surrounding towns.

Our trained team cleans with care and honesty, inside and out: from move-out deep cleans and fridge and oven detailing to windows, gutters, siding and junk removal. Our mission is simple: exceptional, hassle-free cleaning that makes life easier for our clients.
