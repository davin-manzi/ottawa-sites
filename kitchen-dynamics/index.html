About us

Ottawa kitchen and bath design since 1998

Kitchen Dynamics Inc. began in 1998 as a custom kitchen design, renovation and installation business. We bring over 30 years of knowledge of trends, products and materials in the kitchen industry.

Head designer John Searles and our team take every project from first sketch to final install, so you deal with one company instead of a string of contractors. As the exclusive Ottawa-area dealer for Canadiana Cabinets, we offer a full range of cabinet styles.

Questions

FAQ

Can I customize the cabinets to my own needs?

Yes. Our trained designers help you maximize space and storage and build custom cabinets to fit your needs and budget.

What is the warranty?

Canadiana Cabinets carry a limited five-year warranty from the purchase date for the original homeowners. Hinges and drawer slides have a lifetime warranty.

How long until my cabinets arrive?

Lead times depend on finishes, materials and busy periods, but delivery typically takes about 8 to 12 weeks.

Do you have samples I can see?

Yes, we have samples of many styles and colours. If we don't have yours, we can show you a representation or make a custom sample.

Are your cabinets environmentally friendly?

Canadiana finishes meet high European standards, and the plywood and melamine are domestically sourced with no added formaldehyde.

What areas do you serve?

Ottawa, Kanata, Stittsville, Barrhaven, Nepean, Orleans and Carp.
