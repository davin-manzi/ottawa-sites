About us

A Carleton Place framing shop since 2005

Surrounding Memories opened in 2005 as a custom picture framing shop. Over the years we added work by local artists and Canadian companies, and in 2010 we renovated to make room for it all.

Today we're a framing shop, gallery and home décor store in the downtown shopping district of Carleton Place. We'll work with you to find the right frame for your artwork and your décor.

Questions

FAQ

Do I need an appointment for framing?

Framing is by appointment, and you can book online. If we're not already with a client, we're happy to help drop-ins.

What are your store hours?

Giftware is open Tuesday to Saturday, 12:30pm to 5pm. Hours can change around holidays.

Can you fix a frame I already have?

Yes. Book a repair consultation for glass replacement, mat replacement or refitting.

I have more than 5 pieces. How do I book?

Sets of matching prints can go in one booking. For more than 5 different items, book more than one appointment.
