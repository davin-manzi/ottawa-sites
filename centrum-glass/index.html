About us

A family glass shop in Stittsville

Centrum Glass & Door is a family owned and operated business that sells and installs energy-efficient standard and custom doors and windows in Stittsville, Ottawa and surrounding communities.

We're a tightknit group who care about quality. When you call, you talk to a live person. We use only high quality products, finish each project quickly, efficiently and safely, and make sure you're completely happy with the result before we leave.

Questions

FAQ

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We give free estimates at your home or business.

Can you replace just the glass in a foggy window?

Yes. We do sealed unit and glass replacements, as well as full window replacement.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes. We specialize in commercial installations, including storefront windows, curtain wall, skylights, glass walls and security film, and we also do residential work.

Can you make custom glass pieces?

Yes. We cut and fabricate custom shower enclosures, table tops, shelves, mirrors and glass railings, and do sandblasted etching.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm.

How can I pay?

We accept cash or cheque.
