About us

Ottawa's appliance repair team for over 20 years

Appliance Surgeons has provided expert appliance repair to Ottawa residents for over 20 years. We repair all major brands, from Whirlpool, Maytag and Kenmore to LG, Samsung, Bosch and KitchenAid.

FAQ

Is there a warranty on repairs?

Yes. We offer a full 1-year warranty on parts and labour.

Which brands do you repair?

All major brands, including Whirlpool, Maytag, Kenmore, KitchenAid, GE, Frigidaire, LG, Samsung, Bosch, Inglis, Amana and many more.

What areas do you cover?

All of Ottawa, including Kanata, Stittsville, Nepean, Barrhaven, Manotick, Gloucester, Orleans, Cumberland, Rockland and Navan.

Do you repair gas appliances?

Yes. We are specialists in gas appliances, including gas stoves and ranges.

Do you work with landlords and property managers?

Yes. Property owners and managers are welcome, and we service many rental properties across Ottawa.

What should I have ready when I contact you?

The type of appliance, the make, model and serial number, its age, and a description of the problem.
