Ottawa heating and cooling since 1993

Do you give free estimates?

Yes, for furnace and air conditioner replacements.

All of our labour and parts come with a 1-year warranty. Rheem furnaces also come with a 10-year parts warranty.

You can call us 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Do you serve Gatineau?

Yes. We serve Ottawa, Gatineau and the surrounding areas, and offer service in French at 819-595-3238.

What furnaces do you install?

We install Rheem furnaces, which use a DC motor and two-stage operation for up to 95% efficiency.

Yes. We do gas hook-ups for stoves, dryers, barbecues and tankless water heaters.
