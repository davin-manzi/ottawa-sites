About us

Ottawa renovations since 2010

HomeNplus was founded by Kenny Duprate in 2010 and has grown into a full renovation and building company for homes and businesses across the National Capital Region.

Instead of hiring separate designers, engineers and builders, you get one team. We handle the design, the permits and the paperwork, and the build, and we guarantee every job is completed on time and within budget.

FAQ

What area do you serve?

A 100 km radius around the National Capital Region.

How soon can you give me an estimate?

We aim to be on site for a consultation the same day or within 48 hours, depending on location. Every estimate is in writing.

Who comes out to give the estimate?

One of our qualified sales representatives or the owner.

How long is an estimate good for?

Generally we hold prices for 30 days. When markets are volatile, sometimes only for a week.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry general liability insurance and workers' compensation, and we can provide insurance certificates on request.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, through Financeit.
