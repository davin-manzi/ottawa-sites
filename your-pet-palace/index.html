Questions

FAQ

Can I book an appointment by email?

No. We don't take appointments by email. Please call 613-747-6568 to book.

How far ahead do I need to book?

Because we're busy, we book about 4 to 6 weeks in advance. You can ask to join our cancellation list.

Do I need an appointment for a nail trim?

No. Nail trims are done without an appointment, while you wait.

Do you groom cats?

Yes. Cat grooming includes nail trimming, eye and ear cleaning, a comb-out or shave, and a shampoo when possible.

What's included in a full dog groom?

Clipping, comb-out, ear cleaning, nail trimming, and two shampoos with conditioner.

What are your hours?

Tuesday to Friday 8am to 5pm, and Saturday 8am to 4pm. We're closed Sunday and Monday.
