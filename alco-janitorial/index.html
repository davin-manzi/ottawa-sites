About us

Ottawa cleaners since 1993

Established in 1993, Alco Janitorial has served the greater Ottawa area exclusively from day one. From our 3,000 sq. ft. office and warehouse on Canotek Road, we clean for small businesses, institutions and large commercial facilities.

President Dan Altherr took over from his father in 2004. Our secret is simple: we hire only the most reliable, personable and hard-working frontline staff, train them well, and stand behind the result.

Questions

FAQ

Is my office too small for you?

No office is too small. We'll build a plan that suits your space.

Are your employees bonded and insured?

Yes. All of Alco's employees are bonded and insured.

How much does office cleaning cost?

Weekly cleaning starts at $275/month and daily cleaning at $750/month. Final pricing follows a quick site visit.

Do you use eco-friendly products?

Yes. We put high priority on products that aren't harmful to the environment, and use HEPA filters and microfiber cloths.

How do you know the job is done well?

A route supervisor is assigned to every account and keeps cleaning schedules and logs. We use Orange QC software to monitor quality.

Can you do a one-time clean?

Yes. We'll supply a team for a special event or a one-time cleaning.
