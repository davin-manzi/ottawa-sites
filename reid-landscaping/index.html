About us

Plants and landscape supplies in one stop

Reid Gardens, run by Reid Landscaping & Garden Centre, is Carleton Place's one-stop shop for your yard. Pick out trees, shrubs, perennials and annuals, then load up the soil, mulch and stone to plant them in.

Bring your own truck or trailer and we'll load it, or have it delivered. We're also the west-end distributor for Permacon and Bolduc retaining wall and paving stones.

Questions

FAQ

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver loads from 1 cubic yard up to 10 cubic yards. Call us for delivery rates.

Can I pick up with my own truck or trailer?

Yes. Bring your truck or trailer and we'll load it for you.

Are your trees and shrubs guaranteed?

Hardy (zone 4 or lower) evergreens, trees and shrubs have a one year (12 month) guarantee with your original receipt. Sale items, vandalism and improper care aren't covered, and replacements aren't given before June 1st. Send us photos rather than bringing the plant back.

What's your return policy?

Bring your original receipt within 30 days. Plants can't be returned. Interlock and retaining wall products have a 20% handling fee after inspection.

Do you charge for interlock pallets?

Pallets are $40 each, and you get $35 back when you return them in good condition.

What are your hours?

Fall hours are Monday to Saturday, 8am to 4pm. Summer hours are Monday to Saturday, 8am to 5pm. We're closed Sundays.
