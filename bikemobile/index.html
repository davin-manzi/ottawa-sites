About us

Ottawa's original mobile bicycle service

Forty-four years ago, at nineteen, Peter Westaway hoped to open a small bike repair shop in his mother's garage in Bell's Corners. Local bylaws said no, so he started going to his customers instead, and he's been doing it ever since.

Today Bikemobile is very busy every spring. Ken Upiah joins Peter on jobs when his full-time job allows. Every service ends with a test ride, by the mechanic and by you if you like.

Questions

FAQ

What areas do you cover?

Kanata to Orleans, and Barrhaven/Riverside South is where I am most. Manotick, Carp and beyond if the trip is worthwhile, so get in touch to check.

What is the minimum charge?

$70 for an initial service visit. It's never added on top of tune-up or hourly charges.

Do I need to be home?

No. If I can get to the bikes in your backyard or garage, you can pay later by e-transfer after your test ride.

Is there a discount for several bikes?

Yes. Two tune-ups are $160, three are $220, and four or more are $70 each.

How often does my bike need a tune-up?

If you commute and put hard miles on your bike, at least once a year. Bikes from non-specialty stores should be tuned as soon as possible.

What if something goes wrong after you leave?

If you have any problems with your bike after I leave, I'll return as soon as I'm able.
