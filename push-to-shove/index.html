About us

Building trust with quality work

Push To Shove Inc. is a locally owned company serving the Ottawa region, in winter and summer.

In winter we keep commercial and condo properties clear with plow and salter trucks. The rest of the year we install interlock and sod, seal driveways and take on home renovations. Our aim is simple: do the job once, right the first time, at a reasonable price.
