About us

Meet Scott, owner of GreerCo

I'm Scott, and I started GreerCo in 2010. My career began about 20 years ago repairing mechanical car washes, then commercial small engines, then helping with heavy equipment and agriculture. In 2002 I earned my 310T Truck and Coach Technician's licence.

Starting my own business was the natural next step, so I could work for you directly. I take pride in keeping your machinery in top shape, with as little downtime as possible. Quality work is #1.

Questions

FAQ

Do you come to my site?

Yes. GreerCo offers full mobile service.

What kind of equipment do you repair?

Industrial heavy equipment, vocational trucks, trailers, cranes, knuckle booms, mixers, conveyors, scrapers, ice resurfacers, blowers and commercial lawn equipment.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Scott holds a 310T Truck and Coach Technician's licence.

Do you sell hydraulic hose and fittings?

Yes. We use GATES hydraulic hose and fittings, and stock JIC and pipe fittings in -4, -6, -8, -10 and -12.

What parts brands do you use?

Top quality parts, including Gates, Haldex, Bendix, Spicer, Petro-Canada, SKF, FAG, Timken, Alcoa, Gunite and Loctite.
