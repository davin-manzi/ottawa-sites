About us

A family business built by word of mouth

Norm started the business over three decades ago and built it by word of mouth. Many homeowners in Merrickville have used his services, and his crew has worked as far as Kingston and Quebec. Along the way he trained many people who now run flooring businesses of their own.

It has always been family-run, with Norm leading it. Today his daughter and two sons work alongside him to build new floors, repair and refinish old ones, and bring older homes back to life.
