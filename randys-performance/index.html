About us

Honest, friendly service in Kemptville

Randy's Performance Automotive is a one-stop shop for cars, trucks and RVs, from routine maintenance to complex repairs on all makes and models, with a specialty in hot rods and custom exhaust.

Owner Richard spent a decade honing his skills at the shop before taking it over in 2017. He trained in welding at Kemptville College and earned his mechanic licence through Algonquin College in 2005. Ron has been with the shop since it started, Steve is a licensed technician, and Ellie runs the front desk.
