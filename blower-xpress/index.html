About us

Ottawa's blower truck crew since 2009

Blower Xpress Services has served the Ottawa market since 2009. Our blower trucks place soil, mulch, seed and stone through a 4-inch hose, so the material goes exactly where it's needed, without wheelbarrows or machines on your lawn.

We work on commercial, residential and institutional sites, from planting beds and lawns to City of Ottawa parks, school playgrounds, slopes and green roofs.

Questions

FAQ

How far can the hose reach?

Mulch can be pumped upwards of 250 feet from the road, so no equipment has to drive on your lawn.

What is Terraseeding?

Seed is injected into composted organic soil and blown on as a layer on top of the existing soil, usually 1" to 4" deep depending on the soil and the slope. Any seed mix can be used.

Can you topdress and overseed my lawn at the same time?

Yes. The soil can be injected with seed, so topdressing and overseeding happen in one application, typically ¾" to 1" deep.

How high can you place green roof material?

With lightweight material, upwards of 10 stories. It avoids large traffic closures and the crews needed to load and spread by hand.

Is your playground surfacing up to standard?

Yes. Our playground mulch installation meets IPEMA and ASTM regulations and is used by the City of Ottawa in parks and school playgrounds.

Do you have specifications for my architect or engineer?

Yes. We have spec sheets for Terraseeding, EcoBlanket, Filtrexx erosion control and green roof details.
