Questions

FAQ

Can I replace a broken garage door spring myself?

We don't recommend it. Torsion springs are under extreme tension, and adjusting or replacing one without proper tools and training is genuinely dangerous.

How do I know my spring is broken?

Common signs are a loud bang from the garage, a door that won't open or only opens a few inches, a door that feels very heavy, or a visible 2 to 4 inch gap in the torsion spring.
