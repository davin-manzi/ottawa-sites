Questions

FAQ

When does the snow season start and end?

Seasonal contracts run from November 1 to April 1.

When will you clear my driveway?

Drivers are on call 24/7 and start rounds once 5 cm has fallen. Each round can take up to six hours, with one to three rounds per snowfall, so we can't guarantee an exact arrival time.

Will your equipment damage my interlock?

No. We use tractors with rear-mounted snow blowers and polyurethane blades that are safe for interlock driveways.

Do you clear the ridge the city plow leaves?

Yes. Our drivers clean up the ridge left at the end of your driveway by city plows.

What if we get a lot of snow?

The season includes up to 300 cm. Snow beyond that is billed at $0.95 per cm at the end of the season.

How can I pay?

Cheque, credit card, cash or email transfer.
