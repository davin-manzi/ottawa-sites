About us

We help you get where you want to go

Main Street School of Music was founded on the idea that the best way to learn is through the study and practice of contemporary music. Whether you're looking at a career in music or just want to have fun learning an instrument, we can help you get there.

You get one-on-one guidance from your instructor and the chance to play with other musicians. We cover the core skills, like scales, chords, reading and improvisation, and encourage you to explore other styles and find your own sound.

Questions

FAQ

How much are private lessons?

$30 for a half-hour private lesson, or $60 for an hour. Art and photography lessons are the same price. Lessons are prepaid four weeks in advance.

What are your hours?

Private lessons run 2:30pm to 8:00pm, Monday to Thursday. Earlier or later times can be arranged, and some teachers offer virtual lessons through the week.

Do you teach Royal Conservatory?

Yes. We teach Grade 1 to Grade 8 in piano.

What if I have to cancel a lesson?

Give us at least 24 hours' notice and we'll arrange a make-up lesson. Lessons missed without 24 hours' notice are charged.

How do I pay?

We accept e-Transfer, cash, Interac, Visa and Mastercard.

What experience do your teachers have?

All of our teachers are professional musicians, many with degrees in music.
