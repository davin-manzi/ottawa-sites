About us

Two generations of Lalonde builders

Gerald and Shirley Lalonde started the business in August 1967, doing roofing, renovations and odd jobs. It was registered as Gerald A. Lalonde Contracting in March 1982.

Their son Sean started as an apprentice, became a Licensed Carpenter, and now manages the crews. Sean and Mary Jane became partners in June 2012. Gerald is President, Sean is Vice President, Mary Jane runs the office and Shirley is Administrative Assistant.

Questions

FAQ

What kind of work do you do?

Residential and commercial construction: all types of roofing, siding, decks, gazebos, framing, interior and exterior work, stairs and trim, and turnkey construction.

How long have you been in business?

Gerald and Shirley Lalonde started the business in August 1967. It's now run by two generations of the family.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry liability insurance and workers' compensation, and working-at-heights certification is mandatory for our crew.

Who is on site during my job?

Our licensed carpenters do the work, and a senior representative is on the job regularly to coordinate all trades and keep things on schedule.

Do you help with planning a renovation?

Yes. We help set the budget, schedule and design scope before construction, and offer ideas and recommendations along the way.
