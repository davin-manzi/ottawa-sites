About us

Simply, traditional excellence

Lolacher's Catering started locally in 1963 and now serves Ottawa. We've catered corporate events, weddings, memorials, galas and smaller gatherings.

Our menu is classic, comforting and hearty, and we customize it for every event. We're a family, with an experienced crew committed to simple, traditional elegance.

Questions

FAQ

What events do you cater?

Corporate events, weddings, funerals and celebrations of life, social events, mess dinners and more.

Is there a minimum order?

Buffets need a minimum of 25 guests and breakfast menus 10 guests, priced per person.

Can you handle dietary needs?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options are available on request. Additional fees may apply.

Do you provide serving staff?

Yes, our uniformed serving staff can serve your event. A service charge applies when you request service.

Can I have a tasting?

Yes. Contact us to discuss your event and schedule a tasting.

Are taxes included?

No, prices do not include applicable taxes.
