About us

A family moving company since 1969

Adam's Moving is a family-owned moving company that has proudly served the Ottawa region since 1969. For over 55 years we've moved homes, apartments and businesses across the city.

As a family business we support local initiatives, and we're the official moving company for CHEO's Dream of a Lifetime Lottery.

    FAQ

    Do you give free estimates?

    Yes. Call 613-722-6044 or email contact@adamsmoving.ca for a free estimate.

    What affects the cost of my move?

    Distance, the volume of items, crew size, access conditions and any extra services like packing. We give clear, upfront quotes with no hidden fees.

    How far ahead should I book?

    Book early to secure your moving date and time, ideally two to four weeks ahead, especially in peak season.

    Do you do long-distance moves?

    Yes. We handle both local and long-distance moves.

    Are you insured?

    Yes, we offer fully insured, full-service moving. Ask us about insurance options when you book.

    What are your office hours?

    Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. We're closed Saturdays.
