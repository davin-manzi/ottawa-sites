About us

Beauty for every occasion

Whether it's a wedding, private lessons or date night, LINDAJOY's Oasis offers a complete line of beauty services. A graduate of Versailles Academy in Ottawa, the owner has been a professional cosmetician for over 21 years and a nail technician for over 19.

Her career began with places like the Chateau Laurier and public fashion shows, and she has given seminars in high schools and worked with women's crisis centres and Big Sisters. She uses fine Canadian skin care and professional, cruelty-free cosmetics from Italy.

Questions

FAQ

How do I book?

Call 613-725-5798 or send a message through the contact form. All services are by appointment.

How do wedding bookings work?

The first meeting is one-on-one with the bride. We build a price package together, and a 30% deposit secures your date. Group bookings include a consultation and trial a month before.

How much is bridal makeup?

The bride package is $100 and includes photo makeup, an optional gel nail set with art, a facial and eyebrow arching. Bridesmaids are $60 each.

Do you do Halloween makeup?

Yes. Theatre and Comic-Con makeup starts at $10, $15 with blood and $35 with wax.

What products do you use?

Canadian skin care from Céla and Kedem, and professional, cruelty-free Cinecitta cosmetics from Italy.

Do prices include tax?

Prices for our other services (nails, facials, prom and event makeup) do not include 13% HST.
