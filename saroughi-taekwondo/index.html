About us

Taekwon-Do in Ottawa since 1992

Senior Master Sam Saroughi has been promoting ITF Taekwon-Do in the Ottawa region since 1992. He was promoted to 8th Degree Black Belt in November 2016 by Senior Grand Master Jong Soo Park.

Classes build fitness, confidence and self-defence skills, and teach students to solve problems peacefully. Instructors supervise closely, and sparring is only for advanced students who want to take part.

Questions

FAQ

Will martial arts make my child violent?

No. We teach that martial arts are for self-defence and a last resort, and we focus on solving problems peacefully and building confidence.

My child isn't very athletic. Can they still benefit?

Yes. Classes build coordination, flexibility and strength, and activities are matched to each child's level.

Will my child get hurt?

Any exercise carries some risk, so instructors supervise closely. Sparring is only for advanced students who want to and can spar safely.

How fit do I need to be for an adult class?

Students of almost every fitness level train with us. Classes improve your fitness from wherever you start.

What should I wear to my first class?

A comfortable top and pants that are easy to move in.

Is there a trial?

Yes. A 2 week special trial is $65 plus tax.
