About us

Candid photography, cinematic storytelling

Amar Studios is a wedding photography and videography studio in Stittsville. Amar and his team have refined their craft over nearly two decades, while keeping up with modern cameras and editing.

Our approach is natural and unobtrusive. We blend into your day so we can catch the genuine moments, then tell the story with candid photos and cinematic film.
