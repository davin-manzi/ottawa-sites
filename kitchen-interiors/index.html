About us

Ottawa's custom kitchen people since 1987

Kitchen Interiors has represented established Canadian cabinet manufacturers since 1987. We design and build custom kitchens for Ottawa homes, with no extra charge for custom sizes or modifications.

Our designers start with a free consultation and a 3D rendering, then we take care of the rest: demolition, plumbing and electrical coordination, flooring, painting and installation, including engineers and permits if a wall needs to move.
