About us

Meet Amy, your framer

Walkerworks is a busy, owner-operated framing studio in Stittsville, focused on excellent design and long-lasting quality. Owner Amy is a Certified Picture Framer (CPF) and has made retail framing her career since opening her first shop in Perth in August 2000.

Amy studied painting at Concordia University and holds a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Photography from the University of Ottawa. Appointments mean she can give you and your project her full attention when you arrive.

    Questions

    FAQ

    Do I need an appointment?

    Yes. Walkerworks is by appointment only, Tuesday to Saturday, so Amy can focus on you and your project. If your day is full, Amy is fully booked that day.

    What can you frame?

    Almost anything: artwork in any medium, diplomas, needlework, diamond art, jerseys, photos, posters, canvases, medals, pocket watches, even a teddy bear or a favourite album.

    How much does a jersey frame cost?

    A custom Walkerworks Jerseybox starts at $550 plus HST.

    Can I just ask a quick question first?

    Yes. Email info@walkerworks.ca to discuss a project without booking.

    Can you replace the glass in my frame?

    Yes, glass replacement is one of our services. Bring the frame to your appointment.
