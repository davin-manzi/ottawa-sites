About us

Ottawa auto repair since 1984

Jim Dickinson Auto Tech has been fixing cars on Gladstone Avenue since 1984. We service import and domestic cars, light trucks and SUVs.

Our Class A technicians get in-house training so repairs are done right the first time. We only repair what's necessary, with your approval first, and we'll point out anything else to watch, without pressure.
