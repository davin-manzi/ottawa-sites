About us

A family detailing shop in Ottawa since 2004

JREK Autocare has been Ottawa's choice for professional auto and marine detailing since 2004. It's a family business run by Jon and Emilie, with a main shop at 1638 Carling Avenue and a second location at 6 Antares Drive.

Jon has more than two decades in the industry and oversees technician training and quality control. Our CFEAQ-accredited team is trained in ceramic coatings, paint correction, paint protection film and detailing, and our pop-up trailer brings detailing to you.

FAQ

How long does a ceramic coating last?

From two to nine years or more, depending on the package and how it's maintained. We offer 3, 5 and 9 year coatings.

Is a ceramic coating scratch-proof?

No. It's harder than clear coat and resists very light swirls, but it won't stop rock chips or deep scratches. For impact protection we recommend paint protection film.

Can you detail my car at home or at work?

Yes, for full, interior and exterior details. Paint correction and ceramic coatings are done at our shops.

Do I get a car while mine is in the shop?

Our paint protection film and 3 year ceramic coating packages include a loaner vehicle.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:30am to 4:00pm, by appointment only.

Do you detail boats?

Yes. We do full and exterior boat details and marine ceramic coatings, and we can come to your marina, dock or storage location.
