About us

Small engine service at your home or business

ASER exists to provide honest, reliable home or on-site service and repair for lawn and garden equipment and other small engines. In most cases we do tune-ups, maintenance and repairs right where your equipment is.

Larger jobs, and anything that can't be done on site, come back to our repair shop on Arnott Road in Lanark, where we service equipment from most manufacturers.

Questions

FAQ

Do you come to my house?

Yes. In most cases tune-ups, maintenance and repairs are done at your home or business.

What areas do you cover?

Mobile service to the Lanark, Carleton Place, Almonte and Carp areas, and stand-by generator service across Lanark County.

What's included in a tune-up?

Lubrication, adjustments (belts, carburetor and more), a new spark plug and an oil change, plus blade sharpening on lawnmowers and lawn tractors. Extras depend on the model.

How much is a snowblower tune-up?

$140 plus parts and taxes.

Is it cheaper if I bring it in?

Yes. You get 10% off tune-up specials when you bring your equipment to the shop.

When is the shop open?

Most weekdays we're out on service calls. The shop is open Saturdays in spring and fall/winter. Please call ahead to make sure someone is there.
