About us

Meet Michelle

Best Mobile Pet Grooming is run by Michelle Best, who has groomed pets in their own homes across Ottawa since 2002. She specializes in cats and small dogs.

Michelle trained at the Canine School of Professional Grooming in Ottawa and under award-winning international master groomer Dawn Mullin, and keeps learning through courses and seminars.

Questions

FAQ

Are you taking new clients?

Yes for cats. Michelle isn't taking new dog clients at the moment.

How much does it cost?

Cats are $95 (short hair), $105 (medium) or $110 (long). Lion clips are $115 and mat removal starts at $30. Prices are all-inclusive.

How long does a groom take?

About an hour for a cat and about two hours for a dog.

What do I need to provide?

Just a couple of towels. Michelle brings her own grooming table.

How often should my pet be groomed?

About every 3 months for cats and every 8 weeks for dogs.

How do I pay?

Cash, cheque or e-Transfer. No credit cards.

Which areas do you cover?

Across Ottawa, including Orleans, Gloucester, Nepean, Barrhaven and Bells Corners.
