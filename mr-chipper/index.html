About us

Clearing trees in the Valley since 1999

Mr. Chipper is a small, responsive company based in Kinburn. Since 1999 we've been taking down trees, chipping brush and grinding stumps for homes, builders and businesses across Ottawa West and the Ottawa Valley.

Our family-run crew is fully licensed, bonded and insured. We bring a crane for the challenging trees, chip everything on site, and reuse the wood we remove.

Questions

FAQ

What areas do you serve?

Ottawa West and the Ottawa Valley, including Kanata, Stittsville, Nepean, Carp, Almonte, Arnprior, Pakenham and Pembroke. We serve Eastern Ontario only.

How much does stump grinding cost?

The minimum stump grinding charge is $125, which typically covers 1 to 3 stumps and our travel time.

Do you charge for a consultation?

No. Consultations are free, and we'll give you an actual quote.

Can you remove a tree in a tight spot?

Yes. We remove hazardous branches in hard-to-reach locations, and we have a crane for challenging trees.

What happens to the brush and wood?

Brush is chipped and disposed of in an eco-friendly way, and the wood we remove is reused.

What if a storm brings a tree down?

Call our emergency tree removal line at 613-913-8324.
