About us

Restoring the past for the future since 1994

James Reid founded Keystone Traditional Masonry in 1994. Restoring the Past for the Future has been his passion and motto since day one, and he's on site for every project, in direct contact with you.

The craftsmanship and detail that make Ottawa's heritage buildings special take a skilled hand to restore. Because we understand how these buildings were made, we can often solve problems so they don't come back.

Questions

FAQ

What kind of work do you do?

Heritage restoration, new stone construction and natural stone landscaping, from repointing and chimneys to pillars, fireplaces, walks and walls.

Do you use lime mortar?

Yes. We repoint with heritage lime mortar mixes and match the mortar colour to your building.

Will the owner be on my job?

Yes. Founder James Reid is present on each project and in direct contact with clients.

Can you carve stone?

Yes. Custom stone cutting and carving, including lettering, is part of what we do.

How do I get a quote?

Call 613-253-9786 or send us a project request, and we'll reply with a time to meet.

Do you speak French?

Our lead mason, Benoit Lachapelle, is bilingual.
