About us

A family affair since 1927

Morris Feldberg, a military master tailor from Poland, opened his tailoring business in a tiny corner store on Holland Avenue in 1927. His son Jack took over after the Second World War, and in 1980 Jack's son Sam turned it into a specialty formalwear store.

Today Sam runs the shop with his son Joseph from a 5,000 square foot space on Wellington Street West in Hintonburg, the only independent formalwear store in Ottawa with all inventory on the premises.
