About us

Meet Auntie Shannon

Shannon has been caring for dogs at Auntie Shannon's since 2013. She's a certified dog groomer, has been in dog rescue for some time, and shares her home with 8 rescue dogs of her own.

Guests live cage-free on the farm: yard play every hour, cookie time twice a day, pools in summer and a fireside area with couches and beds. At bedtime dogs pick their own spot, and a few lucky ones even sleep in Auntie's room.

Questions

FAQ

Is there a Meet N' Greet?

Yes, it's mandatory. Dogs that show aggression toward dogs or people can't stay, and very anxious dogs may not suit open-concept boarding.

Does my dog need flea and tick prevention?

Yes. Dogs must be on some form of flea and tick prevention. If fleas are found on arrival, a topical treatment is applied and added to your bill.

Does my dog need to be spayed or neutered?

Adult dogs need to be spayed or neutered to board. Young puppies can stay. Please ask us about your dog's age.

Should I bring toys and blankets?

We suggest leaving personal toys, blankets and beds at home to avoid possessiveness and so nothing special gets damaged.

Will you give my dog medication?

Yes, as long as you provide thorough instructions.

How do I pay?

Interac debit or credit at the time of pick-up.
