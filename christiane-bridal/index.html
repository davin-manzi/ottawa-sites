About us

The best girlfriends you never had

Christiane's Bridal is a tailor shop in Orleans for budget brides and anyone who needs clothes altered. With over 25 years of experience, owner Christiane handles bridal alterations, tailoring, mending, repairs and hemming.

We aim to be supportive and style-confident for women and men alike. Christiane also makes dresses, sometimes wedding dresses from scratch. Come visit us today. All are welcome!

Questions

FAQ

When should I get my wedding dress altered?

Book bridal alterations about two months before the wedding. We also accommodate last-minute and emergency alterations.

How much will my alterations cost?

It depends on the work, the fabric and the number of fittings. We don't give estimates online, but your in-person assessment is complimentary.

How many fittings will I need?

Most brides need 2 to 3 fittings.

Can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome if there are openings. Appointments are recommended, and Saturday appointments can only be booked by phone.

Do you buy used wedding dresses?

No. We do not purchase or resell previously owned items.

Is there parking?

Yes, parking is available on site.
