About us

A family who has cared for animals for decades

Matthews Kennels is family owned and operated by Debbie, Melody and Ken Matthews. Before opening in 2000, we spent eight years with the Humane Society, five years as OSPCA Agents and several years in animal rescue.

Today we care for every breed of dog on our 10-acre property just outside Ottawa, from healthy, active dogs to pets with medical conditions. We work with many local veterinarians, and most of our customers are with us for life.

Questions

FAQ

Can we visit before our dog's stay?

Yes, and we strongly recommend it. Tours are by appointment. Call 613-838-2018.

When can I drop off and pick up?

Monday to Friday 8 to 10am and 5 to 8pm. Weekends 8 to 10am, 1 to 3pm and 5 to 8pm. Closed Christmas Day.

Do you have indoor/outdoor runs?

No. Each guest is taken outside individually for one-on-one time with a caregiver.

Can you give my dog medication?

Yes. We give supplied oral medication and supplements at no extra charge.

What should I bring?

Your dog's food for the full stay plus one or two extra meals, and familiar toys or blankets if you like.

Do you offer grooming or obedience training?

No, we don't offer grooming or obedience training at this time.
