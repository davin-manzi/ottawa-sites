Questions

FAQ

How often should my piano be tuned?

Twice a year is recommended, or at least once a year, depending on how much it's played. New or restrung pianos need tuning more often.

How much does a tuning cost?

$150 in Zone 1, which covers Ottawa, Gatineau and nearby communities, and $175 in Zone 2, including Arnprior, Almonte, Carleton Place and Kemptville.

My piano hasn't been tuned in years. Can it be fixed?

Usually, yes. It may need a pitch raise before a fine tuning, and sometimes more than one session.

Can you fix scratches on a glossy piano?

Yes. Light scratches can be buffed out, and deeper ones need a polyester repair, which is $160 for the first hour and $80 for each extra hour.

Why does my piano go out of tune?

Changes in temperature and humidity are the main cause. Keep it away from sun, drafts and heat, and consider a humidity control system.

When can you come?

Hours are flexible: mornings, afternoons, evenings or weekends.
