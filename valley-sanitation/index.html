    Questions

    FAQ

    How often should my septic tank be pumped?

    Usually every three to five years, and for many homes every three to four. The right timing depends on your tank size, how many people live in the house and how much water you use.

    What does a septic inspection include?

    We locate the tank, expose the lids, check for corrosion, check the inlet and outlet baffles and partition wall, dig test holes in the bed, load test when needed, and give you a written report on site.

    Should I get the septic inspected before buying a house?

    Yes. Replacing a septic system is expensive, so it's worth knowing its condition before you buy.

    Do you handle permits for a new system?

    Yes. We prepare the design and permit, submit it to the municipal agency for you, install to the permit and arrange the inspection.

    Where do you work?

    Across Eastern Ontario, from our base in Kemptville.

    What should I keep out of my septic?

    Bleach, harsh cleaners and pesticides, which kill the good bacteria, plus wipes, diapers and cigarettes. Don't connect a water softener to the system.
