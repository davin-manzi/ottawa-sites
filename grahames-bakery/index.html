About us

A living, working bakery since 1885

Our commercial wood-fired brick oven has been baking in Kemptville since 1885. Cedar is lit each morning, and the heat held in the bricks keeps us baking all day. Rick and Debbie's grandfather started working here in 1939 and the Grahame family bought the bakery in 1960.

Today Rick Grahame and his sister Debbie carry on the family tradition, with Rick's son Wesley learning the business. When the oven's arch gave way in 2009, we rebuilt it brick by brick instead of switching to gas. During the 1998 ice storm, the oven cooked turkeys and meals for the community.
