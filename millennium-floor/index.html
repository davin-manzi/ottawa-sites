About us

A family flooring store in Orleans since 1996

Millennium Floor Covering has been family-owned and operated in Orleans since 1996. Owner Richard Legault and our friendly staff help you pick the right floor in our spacious 5,000 square foot showroom.

We sell and install carpet, hardwood, laminate, tile, vinyl and cork, and we're proud to support our community, including the Ottawa Humane Society, local hockey associations, the Lions Club and the local food bank.

Questions

FAQ

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Call 613-837-6003 for a free estimate.

Do you install what you sell?

Yes. We install carpet, hardwood, laminate, tile, vinyl, luxury vinyl and cork. Carpet installation comes with a 1-year guarantee.

Can you help with an insurance claim?

Yes. We work with insurance companies on inspections, estimates and budgets after fire, smoke, water or vandalism damage.

What brands do you carry?

Brands include Shaw, Mohawk, Beaulieu Canada, Torlys, Preverco, Mont-Royal, Goodfellow, Dubeau, NextFloor and Amorim cork, among others.
