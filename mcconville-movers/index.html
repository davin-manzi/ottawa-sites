About us

Piano movers for more than 50 years

McConville Movers has offered piano moving and delivery in Ottawa, Gatineau and the surrounding area for more than 50 years. We only move pianos, and we handle all makes and models.

Piano stores trust us to deliver to their customers, and we also move pianos for homes, schools, churches and concert halls. Our piano tuner technicians help with the transport, and we offer short- and long-term storage.

FAQ

What kinds of pianos do you move?

Apartment-sized and full-size uprights, and pianos of all makes and models.

What areas do you serve?

Ottawa, Gatineau, Eastern Ontario and Western Quebec.

Can you store my piano?

Yes. We offer short-term and long-term storage that is carefully climatized.

What do you need to know for a quote?

Your pick-up and delivery addresses, the number of stairs inside and outside at each, and the piano's height (upright) or length (grand).

When do you deliver?

Deliveries run Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. The office is open Monday to Friday, 9am to 4pm.

Do you speak French?

Yes. We serve customers in English and French.
