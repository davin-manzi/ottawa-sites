About us

A small studio with a big heart

Artistic Director Miki Whyte founded DanceR Studio in 2007. Since then we've taught dance to the Orleans community, from an introductory class for your two-year-old to an extensive training program for your competition-level teen.

Our teachers include Bryanna Wilson, who holds a Bachelor of Early Learning and Community Development, and Becky Foster, who trained with Northern Ballet's CAT scheme and earned her degree from Rambert School.

Questions

FAQ

How young can my child start?

Tiny Tots is for 2-year-olds, and Early Childhood is for ages 3 and 4.

How much are classes?

For the 2026-2027 season, a 45 minute class is $512 and a 1 hour class is $576, plus HST, with a $30 family registration fee.

Can we try it before committing?

Yes. Ages 2 to 7 can take a three month trial of a 45 minute class for $192 plus HST.

Do you offer family discounts?

Yes. A second dancer gets 10% off classes and a third dancer gets 15% off.

How can I pay?

E-transfer, cheque or cash. Pay in full, in 2 or 3 instalments, or in 9 monthly instalments. Instalments have a 5% financing fee.

Are there classes for adults?

Yes. Sunday evenings we have Adult Tap, Adult Hip Hop and Adult Competitive Hip Hop.
