About us

Nut-allergy-safe baking since 2000

The Cake Shop has been whipping up nut-allergy-safe goodness in Hintonburg since 2000. No nuts are used in any of our products, so everyone at the party can have a slice.

Pick up a cake from our daily selection, or order a custom design for a birthday, baby shower, wedding or religious celebration. Our team is led by Nadine, with Julie in the office and Quincy running the decorating team.

Questions

FAQ

Is everything really nut-free?

Yes. We're a 100% nut-free and peanut-free bakery. No nuts are used in any of our products.

How much notice do you need for a custom cake?

Please give us 7 to 10 days for a custom or tiered cake. Send a photo, the date and the number of servings for a quote.

Do you have gluten-free cakes?

Yes, in vanilla, plus our Lemon Drop. We're not celiac-certified.

What is your cancellation policy?

Custom cakes can be cancelled without penalty with at least 5 days' notice. Daily-selection orders cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are charged 50%.

How do I pay?

A credit card is required to accompany all orders.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 9am to 4pm. Closed Sundays.
