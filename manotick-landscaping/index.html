About us

Manotick landscapers since 2001

Manotick Landscaping Contractors is a locally owned and operated landscaping company on River Road in Manotick. Since 2001, our team has completed hundreds of landscaping projects for residential and commercial customers.

We design and install pool areas, patios, walkways, retaining walls and drainage, repair tired interlock, and lay sod, mulch and plants. Our shop also carries the soil, stone and materials for your own projects.

Questions

FAQ

What areas do you serve?

Manotick, Ottawa, Nepean and surrounding Ontario communities.

When are you open?

April to November, Monday to Saturday, 9am to 5pm.

Can you fix sunken or weedy interlock?

Yes. We pressure wash walkways and patios, re-sand brick and flagstone, lift and relay pavers or flagstone, and straighten sagging walls.

Do you sell landscaping materials?

Yes. Our shop carries mulch, soil, riverstone, gravel and sand, cedar hedging, flagstone, Permacon pavers, edge restraint, landscape cloth and polymeric sand.

Do you rent equipment?

Yes. We rent compactors for interlocking brick installation.
