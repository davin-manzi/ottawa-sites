About us

Ottawa masons since 1991

Bytown Masonry started in 1991 as a small residential operation and grew into residential and commercial work across Ottawa. Our crews have worked on almost every kind of structure, from heritage homes to large retail and industrial complexes.

Today we're a team of seven: four fully certified brick and stone masons and three mason labourers. We work with local suppliers and contractors, and we focus on quality, service and attention to detail.
