About us

Pool care from West Ottawa since 2009

Poseidon Pool Service Ltd. was founded in 2009 and is committed to customer-focused service. We do almost everything except build pools: equipment, repairs, leaks, liners, solar heat, covers and automation.

Owner Jens is a Certified Pool Inspector (since 2015, through the National Swimming Pool Foundation) and a licensed SCUBA diver, so many liner leaks get fixed right in the water. We work with licensed electricians and gas fitters when a job needs them.

Questions

FAQ

What areas do you serve?

Ottawa, Kanata, Carleton Place, Smiths Falls, Arnprior and the surrounding areas.

How do you find a leak?

With electronic leak detection: we pressurize the lines or scan the liner electronically. A leak analysis takes about 20 minutes. Our licensed SCUBA diver can often repair liner leaks on the spot.

Is there a warranty on solar heating?

Ultra-Swim solar collectors carry a 10-year manufacturer's warranty, and our installation has a one-year warranty.

Do solar panels need to face south?

No. East- and west-facing collectors can work well depending on the mounting angle. Solar can also be paired with a gas or electric heater as backup.

Can you inspect a pool before I buy a house?

Yes. We do pre-purchase and new-owner inspections, performed by Jens, a Certified Pool Inspector.

Do you build pools?

No. We do almost everything except building pools.
