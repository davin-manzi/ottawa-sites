Questions

FAQ

How long will it take?

One or two cracks usually take one day. Sealing a whole foundation takes one to two weeks, depending on obstacles.

What equipment do you use to dig?

Small Bobcat excavators to reach the footings, with plywood to protect your lawn. Where space is tight or landscaping must be kept, we use Hydrovac trucks.

What happens to my weeping tile?

When we waterproof, the old weeping tile is replaced with new product, and new clear stone goes on top for drainage.

Who does the locates for gas and hydro?

We do, through Ontario One Call, and we carry liability insurance.

Do I need an engineer for structural cracks?

For structural damage like horizontal cracks, we recommend speaking with a structural engineer before we do the job.
